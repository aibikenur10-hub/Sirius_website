Our Journey

Sirius FTC was founded in 2025 with a small group of robotics enthusiasts. Over the years, we’ve grown to a team of 15 dedicated members passionate about building and programming competition-ready robots. From our first regional competition to winning awards nationally and internationally, our journey is one of innovation, teamwork, and learning.

Mission & Values

  • Innovation: We constantly improve our designs and explore new technologies.
  • Teamwork: Collaboration is at the heart of every project.
  • Learning: We value hands-on experience and mentoring future engineers.
  • Integrity: Compete fairly and support other teams.
